How to read this

The upper panel shows surface elevation divided by initial amplitude. The lower panel shows the bed elevation. A wider transition usually reduces reflection. Nonlinear waves can steepen and change phase; a difference in profiles is not the same as a local height error.

Illustrative grid: 1,000 cells; A=0.2 m, σ=150 m, CFL=0.4; fully wet, static bed and outflow boundaries. Quantitative conclusions and mesh checks are in the report and results CSV files. This is a precomputed offline visualization, not an inundation forecast.
